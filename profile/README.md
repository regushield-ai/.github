# ReguShield AI

**Compliance Intelligence Infrastructure**

ReguShield AI turns operational and transaction data into audit-ready regulatory decisions for EU-regulated businesses.

[Website](https://regushield.ai) · [About](https://regushield.ai/about) · [Founder](https://regushield.ai/founder) · [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/regushield-ai)

## What we are building

Infrastructure for explainable compliance decisions, evidence management and executive reporting:

- Regulatory obligation mapping across AMLA, AMLR, MiCA, DORA, the EU AI Act and the FATF Travel Rule.
- Inherent and residual risk scoring, control effectiveness assessment and evidence-linked reasoning.
- Audit-ready outputs and executive reporting designed for supervisory scrutiny.

Built for FinTech, crypto and CASP businesses, AI companies and organizations entering EU-regulated markets. Qualified compliance professionals retain final judgment.
